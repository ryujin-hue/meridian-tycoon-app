# HOW TO PLAY — Cara Bermain / How to Play / Cara Bermain (ID)

## Bahasa Melayu (MS)

**Meridian Tycoon** — anda ialah CEO. Mula dengan satu operasi kecil, naik 8 tier
syarikat dengan membeli syarikat melalui lelongan. Sesi 5–15 minit, 2–4 kali sehari.

1. **Buka app** → Ringkasan Semalaman: pendapatan offline (maks 8 jam, boleh
   dinaikkan dengan penyelidikan), lelongan selesai (menang/kalah), pergerakan
   CEO saingan, satu berita pasar, dan butang **Kutip**.
2. **Markas**: lihat modal (TM), DP, KM, reputasi, lencana tier. Bina operasi
   dalam slot yang dibuka ikut tier; naik taraf hingga aras 10. Tekan **Kutip
   Semua** untuk mengutip pendapatan terkumpul (gaji harian & 25% bayaran hutang
   didebit automatik).
3. **Pasar**: pilih kategori (Mid-market, Rare, Mega, Legacy), lihat julat anggaran
   (±8% NT; kemahiran CTO *Market Scan* dedah nilai tepat 24 jam). Mula lelongan:
   harga mula 95% NT, tempoh 8 jam masa nyata, diselesaikan walaupun anda offline.
4. **Bida**: preset +5%/+10% atau jumlah tersuai. Yuran 2% setiap bidaan. Boleh
   guna leverage sehingga 40% harga (faedah 8% seminggu, dihadkan 2× pendapatan
   harian bersih). Skrin pengesahan memaparkan tunai, leverage dan yuran.
5. **Menang**: syarikat menyumbang 35% pendapatan kasarnya sebagai bersih; naik
   taraf integrasi (L1–L5, +10% setiap peringkat) di tab Syarikat. **Kalah**:
   sasaran muncul semula selepas 48 jam (reputasi sedikit turun).
6. **Staf**: 5 peranan (COO, CFO, CMO, CTO, HR) — upah dari pool, gaji harian
   ikut tier didebit semasa Collect, kemahiran aktif ada cooldown.
7. **Research**: 3 pohon (Operasi, Pasaran, Merger) dibeli dengan DP.
8. **Pitch pelabur**: pilih sudut (Growth/Safety/Story), menang → DP.
9. **Naik Tier**: penuhi Kapasiti + Reputasi + Modal dan miliki ≥1 syarikat tier
   sasaran → sahkan → sinematik peta empayar.
10. Hutang dibayar balik automatik 25% setiap Collect; boleh bayar bila-bila masa.

F2P yang adil: tiada tembok berbayar, tiada iklan, tiada loot box. KM hanya
kosmetik/keselesaan (KM Shop "Coming soon"). Data game dikemas kini dari repo
data apabila ada internet; game berfungsi penuh offline.

## English (EN)

**Meridian Tycoon** — you are the CEO. Start with one small operation and climb
8 company tiers by acquiring companies through auctions. Sessions of 5–15
minutes, 2–4 times a day.

1. **Open the app** → Overnight Summary: offline income (max 8 h, extendable via
   research), finished auctions (won/lost), rival CEO movement, one market news,
   and a **Collect** button.
2. **HQ**: check capital (TM), DP, KM, reputation, tier badge. Fill operation
   slots unlocked by tier; upgrade up to level 10. Tap **Collect All** to pocket
   accrued income (daily salaries & 25% debt repayment auto-debited).
3. **Market**: browse categories (Mid-market, Rare, Mega, Legacy); estimates are
   a ±8% range around NT (CTO *Market Scan* reveals exact value for 24 h). Start
   an auction: opening price 95% of NT, 8 real-time hours, resolves even offline.
4. **Bid**: presets +5%/+10% or a custom amount. 2% fee per bid. Leverage up to
   40% of price (8% weekly interest, capped at 2× net daily income). The confirm
   screen shows cash, leverage and fees.
5. **Win**: the company contributes 35% of its gross income as net; raise its
   integration level (L1–L5, +10% each) in the Company tab. **Lose**: the target
   returns after 48 h (slightly lower reputation).
6. **Staff**: 5 roles (COO, CFO, CMO, CTO, HR) — hire from the pool, daily
   tier-bound salary debited on Collect, active skills have cooldowns.
7. **Research**: 3 trees (Operations, Market, Merger) bought with DP.
8. **Investor Pitch**: pick an angle (Growth/Safety/Story); winning grants DP.
9. **Tier Up**: satisfy Capacity + Reputation + Capital meters and own ≥1 company
   of the target tier → confirm → empire-map cinematic.
10. Debt is auto-repaid 25% of every Collect; repay anytime.

Fair F2P: no paywalls, no ads, no loot boxes. KM is cosmetic/comfort only
(KM Shop "Coming soon"). Game data updates from the data repo when online;
fully playable offline.
